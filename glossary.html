Course Glossary

A searchable academic glossary covering 599 electrical, electronics, measurement, PCB, embedded-systems, protocol, RF, test, safety, and supporting physics terms used throughout the course. Use it for a quick definition, to decode an acronym or symbol, or to review vocabulary by category and course week without leaving Alfred.

How the Glossary supports the Classroom

Important technical vocabulary is bolded with a dotted underline in lessons. On a mouse/keyboard device, hover or keyboard-focus a term for a concise definition card. Click the term—or tap it on a touchscreen—to open its full entry here.

Academic definition policy: each entry gives a plain-English definition, a more technical definition, pronunciation, accepted aliases/notation, course-week mapping, cross-references, and an authoritative terminology source. The linked source controls where a specialized standard uses a narrower definition.
