## Adversarial findings resolved in v16.1

1. **Partial-data risk:** completion overlays are loaded by every page that consumes the affected curriculum, question, or lab data.
2. **Coverage illusion:** every row has an explicit lesson section, guided and independent action, assessment route, mastery route, and practical evidence statement; mapping alone is explicitly not called mastery.
3. **CETa threshold ambiguity:** ETA’s official 75% threshold is separated from Alfred’s two-current-full-runs-at-85% readiness rule.
4. **Attempt-history bug:** assessment attempts retain the compact bank revision needed to count two current qualifying runs.
5. **Week 18 fragmentation:** one cumulative gate now spans baseline through report and includes controlled fault recovery.
6. **Safety traceability:** C1.6 explicitly requires the hazard and correction to be recorded and the safe state independently verified before testing resumes.
7. **Calendar corruption:** presentation text and the ICS are normalized without date, event, or UID drift; the malformed Week 1 library host is corrected.
8. **Menu/study desktop regression:** the bounded More menu and corrected Study hero grid remain in the release and are included in responsive QA.
9. **Offline stale-version risk:** the service worker uses a new v16.1 cache and includes all completion-layer dependencies.

## Honest boundaries

No static website can guarantee certification, employment, or mastery. This release provides a complete route and evidence system. The learner must do the retrieval, calculations, labs, corrections, cumulative project, physical repetitions, and two qualifying CETa practice runs. Alfred questions are original and AI-assisted/editorially reviewed, not official ETA items or externally validated psychometrics. External standards and links can change. Any mains, high-energy, RF-transmit, elevated, chemical, or otherwise hazardous work remains outside the unsupervised course boundary.
