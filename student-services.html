Academic Support

Office Hours Protocol

When a concept or lab blocks you, bring the problem into ChatGPT as if you were attending instructor office hours.

  1. State the week and assignment.
  2. Show what you expected to happen.
  3. Show what actually happened—code, measurement, error, photo, or waveform.
  4. Explain what you already tried.
  5. Ask for diagnosis or teaching, not merely the final answer.

Engineering Laboratories

Lab Safety

  • Keep personal bench work low voltage and current limited.
  • Confirm polarity, supply settings, and expected current before power-up.
  • Understand DMM current-mode connections before measuring current.
  • Respect earth-referenced oscilloscope probe grounds.
  • Use ESD precautions for sensitive devices.
  • Disconnect power before continuity/resistance checks unless the instrument procedure explicitly says otherwise.

Official manufacturer guidance controls whenever a tool or device has specific safety requirements.
