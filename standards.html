Mastery Model

How a standard moves

Only substantive questions from the current reviewed evidence revision count as validated auto-graded competency evidence. Historical mixed-bank evidence remains visible for revalidation. Automatic status uses validated accuracy, repeated sessions, retention streak, recency, and distinct current-bank breadth; the system does not relabel Foundation items as higher-difficulty evidence. A standard with fewer than two distinct current reviewed items is labeled Evidence Limited and cannot automatically claim Mastered until the bank expands. Subject-specific semantic/performance work remains separate evidence and does not inflate the auto-graded status. Incorrect answers return after about 2 days; successful retrieval expands toward 7, 21, then 45 days.
