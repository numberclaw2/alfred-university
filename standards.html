Mastery Model

How a standard moves

Only substantive v14.2 questions count as competency evidence. Foundation questions can start a standard, but Proficient/Mastered status also requires repeated sessions and intermediate/advanced evidence. Incorrect answers return after about 2 days; successful retrieval expands toward 7, 21, then 45 days.
