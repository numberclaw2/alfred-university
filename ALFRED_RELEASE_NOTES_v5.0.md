# Alfred University v5.0 — Consolidated Academic Reinforcement and Full-Syllabus Rehearsal

**Built from exact deployed v4.9 source**, commit `aef85eea87a7319568b372ea8784896b94cf55a5`. This is a consolidated academic release candidate, not an ETA endorsement or independently validated prediction of exam success.

## Academic improvements

1. **23 detailed instructional additions** in safety, electrical math, loaded networks, meters, reactance and energy, transformer/relay action, semiconductor bias and SCRs, power-supply ripple, op-amp gain/bandwidth/slew, workmanship, sequential logic, memory, instruments, RF and communications, and technical documentation. These were placed in 23 actual lesson pages, not in a separate detached encyclopedia.
2. **10 longer question explanations** across existing scored instructional records (the 8 selected IDs have 10 placements). Existing prompt, choices, answer indices, objective tags and IDs are unchanged.
3. **Two new 100-question full-syllabus rehearsals**, Forms C and D, using **200 distinct, previously available instructional questions**; across them all **161 standalone technical objectives absent from fresh Forms A/B** are explicitly represented at least once. The remaining ETA 2.5.1 entry is an EM5 cross-reference, not a separate technical question. The C/D banks are familiar lesson items—**not new, independent exams**; no readiness gate can be passed from them.
4. **Eight new mandatory chapter-based book checkpoints**, increasing Sixth Edition chapter-reading presence from 18/28 instructional modules to **26/28**. No contrived assignments were made for M10 or M19, where the vintage Sixth Edition is not a good match to the newer syllabus. New book assignments cite source chapter boundaries and clear recall tasks, explicitly without claiming passage-by-passage validation.
5. **Source-to-syllabus 22-chapter crosswalk** and audit records preserve verified chapter starts/ends while recording that a complete factual comparison of every book passage and every ETA competency remains an academic-review requirement.

## Learning and assessment design

- The new rehearsals are self-paced, in groups of ten, with device-local autosave and scored explanations only at the end. Both are available in **Assessments**. Their results are stored in the existing v1 local academic record's `review` field, with explicit `readinessEligible:false`. Their scoring does not alter the two independent editorial A/B forms or module mastery gates.
- Publisher errata and ETA 2025 competencies remain the primary authority where a historical study-guide statement may be wrong or outdated.
- Existing optional offline mode, header Cloud Sync indicator, progress keys, exam responses and PDF remain unchanged; only the educational source JS, runtime UI, PWA cache and documentation are replaced.

## Explicit limitations

**No responsibly defensible pass guarantee is possible.** The real ETA examination bank, its current item-weighting and its predictive relationship to Alfred's internal performance are unavailable. The 234-page publisher book is image-based; the 22 chapter boundaries and selected instructional passages were inspected, but this package is **not** an independent, assertion-by-assertion technical review of every page and illustration. The original Forms A/B still sample the same 100 objectives and require independent subject-matter expert calibration. The new C/D rehearsals reuse previously learned instructional questions. These facts remain visible rather than being disguised as completed certification validation.
