ALFRED UNIVERSITY v16.1 — INSTRUCTIONAL COMPLETION — FLAT UPLOAD

The complete website contains 78 files directly at the ZIP root.
No nested folders. No frontend build. No Worker or D1 migration.

1. Open Progress on the existing site and choose Export Backup.
   Save your Recovery / Student Sync Key separately; it is not in the export.
2. Extract Alfred-University-v16.1-INSTRUCTIONAL-COMPLETION-FINAL-FLAT.zip on your computer.
3. Open the existing numberclaw2/alfred-university repository at the root,
   where index.html already lives.
4. Choose Add file > Upload files and select ALL 78 extracted files together.
   Do not upload the ZIP, a wrapper folder, or duplicates ending in (1).
5. Commit replacements. Keep the existing GitHub Pages branch/root setting.
6. After Pages deployment finishes, close old Alfred tabs and reopen the site.
   Release Notes should show v16.1 at the top: “Instructional Completion — Every Requirement Gets a Teaching Path.”

QUICK CHECK AFTER UPLOAD
- At 100% zoom, click More. Every destination, including Release Notes, must
  be reachable. The menu scrolls internally when the screen is short.
- Open Study and start/resume. Confirm your existing Progress records remain.
  Do not mark real work complete just to test the website.
- Open Learn / Classroom. Choose a week and confirm the seven-stage path, the in-house
  CETa and Career lessons, required knowledge checks, and the weekly mastery gate.
- Open Calendar, switch Month / Week / Agenda, and open an event.
- Open Documents and confirm the v16.1 Curriculum & Instruction Plan opens as a Word file.
- Open the Academic System Guide and confirm the live workflow is still present.
- If using Cloud Sync, check its status on your normal device.

DO NOT CLEAR BROWSER SITE DATA AS A NORMAL UPDATE STEP.
That can erase local progress and the stored key. The service worker updates
its own cache without requiring a learner-data reset. Do not erase the cloud
record as a troubleshooting step.

Historical quiz scores remain. Some mastery rows may say Revalidation Needed
because earlier aggregates mixed in later-retired questions. Current Classroom
checks and assessments build current evidence; completed coursework is not reset.

The private CETa booklet, Recovery Key, exported backups and QA files must not
be uploaded to GitHub. The separate audit report is for you and need not be
uploaded. Old unused mirror scripts and hotfix notes can remain in the repo;
