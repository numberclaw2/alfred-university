## Executive finding

The release contains **31 of 31 weeks** and **62 of 62 required primary lessons**. Every week has one CETa lesson and one Career lesson. Every lesson contains a purpose, prerequisite activation, retrieval task, Alfred-authored mechanism explanation, representation/calculation guidance, evidence rule, full diagnostic worked chain, misconception repair, guided practice, independent transfer, technician connection, troubleshooting sequence, required knowledge check, teach-back, and traceability section.

The lesson depth layer ranges from **1,528 to 6,612 words** before counting the original v16 lesson, required check, media guidance, weekly application, and mastery gate. Word count is only a completeness screen; the audit also checks the required instructional components and exact row routes.

## Why the lesson order supports learning

- Each week begins with destination and prerequisites, which reduces hidden assumptions and executive-function demand.
- The CETa lesson builds terminology, mechanisms, diagrams, calculations, and safe decisions.
- The Career lesson applies the same ideas to instruments, hardware, firmware, test, troubleshooting, documentation, or interview evidence.
- External media follows the in-house explanation and includes a purpose plus an accessible text alternative.
- Worked → guided → independent practice fades support instead of asking for unsupported performance immediately.
- Required checks provide immediate correction; weekly mastery and later retrieval revisit knowledge after delay and in changed contexts.
- Progressive disclosure keeps row-level traceability and long reference material available without placing it in the learner’s first visual path.
- Quiet Mode, Focus Mode, resume state, one next action, explicit Required/Supplemental labels, no streak penalty, and bounded repair preserve depth while reducing attention friction.
