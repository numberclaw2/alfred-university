Purpose

Structure serious independent learning like serious academic work.

Alfred University is a fictional personal learning environment built around AU-ESET 301. Its university identity gives the work a consistent home for lessons, labs, assessments, projects, schedules, progress, and study support.

The purpose is practical: create enough structure and continuity that independent electronics learning behaves like a coherent program instead of a pile of disconnected resources.

Release-specific repairs and audit history are intentionally kept out of this permanent explanation. Use Release Notes for what changed and Deployment Notes for how the platform is hosted and maintained.

What the framing means

Fictional institution. Real learning work.

The university presentation is an organizational device, not a claim of accreditation or academic standing.

Fictional

Alfred University, its school/department identity, mascot, institutional history, academic branding, and internally generated university-style documents are part of the personal study environment.

Real

The electronics concepts, hands-on practice, linked third-party educational resources, assessments, projects, evidence-building, and career-transition work are used for actual learning and skill development.
