## onetTech — O*NET 17-3023.00 — Electrical and Electronic Engineering Technologists and Technicians
- **Organization:** U.S. Department of Labor / O*NET
- **Role:** Current occupational task benchmark
- **URL:** https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/17-3023.00
- **How used:** Updated 2026. Covers design/build/repair/adjust/modify, schematics, test interpretation, equipment selection, records and related technician work.

## blsTech — Electrical and Electronic Engineering Technologists and Technicians
- **Organization:** U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics
- **Role:** Current occupational duty benchmark
- **URL:** https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineering-technicians.htm
- **How used:** Assembly, test, calibration, repair, preventive maintenance, diagnostic devices, quality control and reporting.

## l3harrisAlpharetta — Electronics Test Technician C — Alpharetta, GA

- **Role:** Current role benchmark
- **URL:** https://careers.l3harris.com/en/job/alpharetta/electronics-test-technician-c/4832/100762465696
