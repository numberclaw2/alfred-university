Academic Philosophy

Understand it. Measure it. Debug it. Explain it.

The Alfred engineering standard is built around demonstrations of competence rather than passive completion.

01

Electronics Foundations

DC/AC circuits, components, semiconductors, power supplies, amplifiers, logic, schematics, and troubleshooting.

02

Embedded Computing

C, state machines, STM32, GPIO, SWD, UART, I2C/SPI, sensors, timing, and fault handling.

03

Engineering Laboratory

DMM, bench supply, oscilloscope, logic analyzer, solder/rework, staged bring-up, and measurement discipline.

04

Test & Validation

Python automation, repeatable PASS/FAIL checks, logging, fault injection, root cause, regression, and reports.

Applied Electronics Laboratory

The lab is where concepts become engineering judgment.

Every major technical block in AU-ESET 301 eventually reaches hardware, code, measurements, or a troubleshooting problem. That is intentional: the goal is to leave with evidence you can defend in an interview.
