Weekly Planning

Three things that matter this week.

Open complete Classroom →
1

Review reached material

Reread and revisit what Classroom has already taught before worrying about material you have not reached.

2

Use more than one pass

Read, watch, inspect the visual, flip a flashcard, and say the idea in your own words. Repetition with different representations helps the material settle.

3

Check memory after study

Active recall, practice, and teach-back are powerful checks—but they come after you have had a fair chance to learn and review the material.

4

Park rabbit holes

Save interesting side questions and site issues instead of letting them pull you away from the current learning objective.

Recovery Path

You’re stuck. Pick one smaller move.

Need deeper help? Use the Office Hours protocol

Bring the problem into ChatGPT like an instructor office-hours visit. Give enough evidence to diagnose the gap instead of asking only for the final answer.

  1. State the week and assignment.
  2. Show what you expected to happen.
  3. Show what actually happened—code, measurement, error, photo, or waveform.
  4. Explain what you already tried.
  5. Ask for diagnosis or teaching, not merely the final answer.
