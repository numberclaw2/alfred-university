# AU-ESET 301 v16.3.39 — Final Beginner-Dependency Audit

## Verdict

**PASS for the v16.3.39 dependency-first Career reconstruction.** The 31-week Career strand now declares what CETa knowledge is available, what Career prerequisite is activated, what new Career learning is introduced, and what later work depends on it. Official CETa competency identities/wording are not rewritten by this patch.

## Acceptance gates

- **First-use gate — PASS:** high-risk professional tools/concepts are introduced before their reconstructed dependent procedures.
- **Dependency gate — PASS:** every Career lesson has an explicit prerequisite statement and every teaching section has a `buildOn` dependency.
- **Future-concept gate — PASS:** the Week 3 measurement lesson no longer relies on UART/serial-domain knowledge taught much later.
- **Beginner-explanation gate — PASS:** the high-risk lessons were reconstructed around mental model → parts/states → known-good behavior → worked/guided/independent use.
- **I DO / WE DO / YOU DO gate — PASS:** all reconstructed high-risk lessons retain worked examples, guided practice, and independent transfer; key lessons also make the progression explicit inside the teaching body.
- **Healthy-before-broken gate — PASS:** first-power, measurement, MCU bring-up, bus integration, and automated test sequences establish expected behavior before controlled faults.
- **Tool gate — PASS:** bench supply/DMM, oscilloscope, Git, C/toolchain, STM32/SWD, Python/serial, logic analyzer, spectrum analyzer, and automated-test infrastructure are explicitly onboarded before being treated as invisible infrastructure.
- **Cross-track gate — PASS:** Career activates CETa foundations rather than duplicating them, while providing additional depth where Career needs more than CETa supplies.
- **Source-provenance gate — PASS:** every Career standard and each weekly Career curriculum block has named provenance and exact source references in the accompanying matrices/ledger.
