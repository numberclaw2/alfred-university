Career transition:
  • 78 Alfred career-transition standards.
PASS — all 78 have substantive graded-question coverage.
PASS — every Career standard has at least 2 distinct graded questions.

CETa bank-breadth safeguard:
  • 235 assessable CETa rows currently have one distinct substantive graded item attached directly
    to that exact row.
  • v14.2 therefore prevents a one-item standard from receiving a Mastered label solely by repeating
    the same question. A one-item standard may accumulate evidence and reach Proficient, but Mastered
    requires at least two distinct substantive bank items plus the normal repeated-session,
    accuracy, recency, and Intermediate/Advanced evidence requirements.
  • Foundation-only evidence cannot produce Proficient or Mastered status.

This guard is intentional: it prevents the competency dashboard from claiming more certainty than the
question bank can support.

  • per-record headroom on the largest record: 14,322 characters (~71.6%)
  • full-sync request headroom: 197,722 bytes (~38.6%)

RESULT — no Cloudflare Worker change and no D1 schema migration are required for v14.2.

Known Cloud Sync behavior retained from the existing architecture:
If two devices modify different fields inside the same `week:<n>` record while both are offline,
the record-level newest-write merge can cause one whole week record to win. This is an existing
same-record/offline conflict behavior, not a v14.2 regression. Normal connected use syncs changes
after a short delay and greatly reduces the chance of that edge case.
