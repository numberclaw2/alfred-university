Course Description

A professional bridge from adjacent technical experience into electronics and embedded hardware.

AU-ESET 301 develops practical electronics, programming, measurement, troubleshooting, and documentation skills for permanent full-time bridge roles such as R&D Engineering Lab Technician, Hardware Test/Validation Technician, Hardware Integration/Test Technician, and related electronics positions.

The course intentionally does not require full embedded-engineer mastery before the career transition. RTOS, CAN, PCB design, Linux, C++, and deeper HIL work remain role-driven development rather than universal blockers.

Academic Phases

The 31 weeks are one connected engineering journey.

The phase view gives you the big picture; the weekly curriculum below gives you the exact sequence.

Phase I · Weeks 1–5

Electrical Foundations

Safety, Ohm/Watt, DC networks, instruments, AC behavior, and the first mastery gate.

Phase II · Weeks 6–13

Electronics & Troubleshooting

Reactance, components, semiconductors, supplies, amplifiers, digital logic, soldering, schematics, and fault isolation.

Phase III · Weeks 14–18

Embedded Core

C, state machines, STM32, SWD, UART, Python serial, I2C/SPI, sensors, and logic-analyzer proof.

Phase IV · Weeks 19–23

CETa Breadth & Readiness

Cabling, RF/telecom breadth, cumulative review, CETa readiness, and Project 1 design/setup.

Phase V · Weeks 24–29

Capstone & Role Proof

Hardware bring-up, interfaces, firmware architecture, Python test automation, fault injection, and R&D lab evidence.

Phase VI · Weeks 30–31

Career Launch

Portfolio, resumes, interview defense, bridge-job green-light, applications, and market feedback.
