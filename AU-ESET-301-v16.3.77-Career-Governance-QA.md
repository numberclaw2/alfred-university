# AU-ESET 301 — v16.3.77 Career Governance Reconstruction QA

## Scope
This release changes the **governing Career standards/curriculum architecture**, not the lesson content of Weeks 1–31.

## Acceptance result
- Existing Career standards retained: **81**
- New Career standards: **12** (C14 + C15)
- Total Career standards: **95**
- Career domains: **15**
- Proficiency model: **L0–L4**
- 31 weeks assigned explicit occupational targets and required artifacts: **31/31**
- Only Week 2 is initially marked `VERIFIED_PASS`; all other weeks remain explicitly remediation-required.
- Week 2 receives explicit occupational-task/evidence/benchmark/physical-boundary metadata so it passes the new structural gate without changing its teaching content.
- New standards do not pretend to have assessment evidence; they begin as required readiness standards awaiting teaching/performance routes.

## Critical policy repairs
1. CETa knowledge can no longer satisfy Career competence.
2. Reading/watching cannot satisfy a procedural standard.
3. Simulation cannot satisfy physical proficiency.
4. A later lab cannot excuse missing pre-lab teaching.
5. Role-proof readiness requires authentic artifacts.
6. Quality/workmanship requires external acceptance criteria and authority boundaries.
7. Instrument use requires validity/status checks, not merely a reading.
8. A week cannot be declared Career PASS unless the hard gate passes.

## Known baseline backlog
This release intentionally does **not** hide the backlog. Week 3, Week 4, Week 12 and Week 29 remain P0. Weeks 1 and 5–28/30–31 remain remediation-required at their listed priority until their Career pages are upgraded and re-audited.
