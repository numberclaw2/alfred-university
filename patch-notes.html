Release logging standard

Starting with v16.3.10, every website update must include a Release Notes entry before the upload package is delivered. Each entry records the reason for the change, what was added/changed/removed, and the exact files added, modified, or removed.

The pre-v16.3.10 human-readable history contains 42 releases from v1.x through v16.3.9; v16.3.10 and later entries follow the permanent exact-file logging rule.
