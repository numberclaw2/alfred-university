Hardware Access Policy

No hardware? Keep moving.

Virtual and Physical are two routes to the same learning objective. You do not need to complete both routes. A virtual lab counts for academic completion unless the lab is explicitly marked as a tactile skill that still needs physical verification later.

✅ Virtual Complete — course objective completed in simulation✅ Physical Complete — objective completed on real hardware🔄 Virtual Complete / Physical Optional — revisit later for hands-on repetition🛠 Physical Verification Later — tactile skill such as actual soldering still needs real practice eventually
